Welcome to Café XYZ

A new place to gather in the neighborhood

Café XYZ is a new local café built for the community. Stop by for freshly pulled espresso, house-made pastries, and a warm seat to catch up with friends, get some work done, or meet your neighbors. Everyone is welcome, whether you are a longtime regular or visiting for the first time.

This site is our digital cork board. Check back often for our latest menu items, weekly deals, and upcoming events.
