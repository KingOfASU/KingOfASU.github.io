Our Menu

Made fresh every morning

Everything at Café XYZ is made to order or baked fresh each morning. Our espresso drinks are pulled from locally roasted beans, and our pastries come out of the oven before we open the doors. Look over the menu below, and ask our baristas about this week's seasonal special.

We also sell our house coffee beans by the bag, so you can take the café home with you.
